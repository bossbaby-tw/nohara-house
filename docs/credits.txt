Custom 3D geometry and procedural surface textures created for this experience.
Character design references: official family character guide at https://www.tv-asahi.co.jp/shinchan/character/ and Futabasha's https://manga-shinchan.com/character . Reference artwork is not bundled in the game.
Kasukabe Afternoon: original instrumental game score composed for this experience, synthesized locally in the browser. This is not an official Shin-chan recording.
Optional official music video: Ora wa Ninkimono (India version), official movie dance video at https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ePB7BXSCTaM , sourced from https://shinchan-movie.com/2025/20250529/news/ .
